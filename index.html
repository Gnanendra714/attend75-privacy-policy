Attend75 Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information Attend75 uses, where it is stored, and how third-party services such as advertising may process information.

1. About Attend75

Attend75 is a personal attendance and timetable management application.

The app is designed to help students manage attendance records, subjects, timetables, attendance targets, reminders, reports, and related study information.

Attend75 does not require you to create an account or log in to use its core features.

2. Information You Enter

You may enter information into Attend75 such as:

  • Your name or profile name
  • Subject names
  • Attendance records
  • Timetable information
  • Classroom or room information
  • Attendance targets
  • Notification preferences
  • Other information you choose to store inside the app

This information is primarily stored locally on your device for the app's functionality.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

Attend75 uses local device storage for information required by the app.

This may include your profile information, subjects, attendance records, timetable information, notification preferences, attendance targets, reports, and other app settings.

Attend75 does not require an Attend75 account or an Attend75 cloud server for these core features.

If you uninstall the app or clear its application data, locally stored information may be deleted by Android.

4. Camera and Timetable Scanning

Attend75 may request access to your device camera when you choose to scan a timetable.

The camera is used only after you choose to use the timetable scanning feature.

Attend75 does not use the camera continuously or in the background.

Images captured for timetable scanning are used to perform the requested timetable recognition process. Attend75 does not intentionally upload your timetable photographs to an Attend75 server.

5. Gallery and Image Selection

If you choose to select a timetable image from your device gallery, Attend75 accesses the image you select for the purpose of timetable scanning.

Attend75 does not intentionally upload selected timetable images to an Attend75 server.

You remain in control of which image you choose to provide to the app.

6. Optical Character Recognition (OCR)

Attend75 uses optical character recognition (OCR) technology to help identify timetable information from scanned timetable images.

The OCR functionality is used to recognize information such as days, times, subjects, and room details.

The OCR processing used by Attend75 is performed on the device using the application's integrated machine-learning functionality.

7. Notifications

Attend75 can provide optional notifications such as class reminders, attendance-related reminders, daily summaries, missed-attendance notifications, recovery guidance, and target guidance.

Notification preferences are controlled by you through the app's Settings.

Android notification permission may be requested when required by your version of Android.

8. Advertising

Attend75 uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as the device IP address, user interactions with the app and advertisements, diagnostic information, and device or advertising identifiers, depending on the SDK configuration and applicable privacy settings.

This information may be used for purposes including advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and service improvement.

The data practices of Google Mobile Ads are governed by Google's applicable privacy and advertising policies.

You can manage certain advertising and privacy choices through your device settings and any consent mechanisms provided by Attend75 where required.

9. Information Shared with Third Parties

Attend75 does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when required for app functionality or advertising.

The main third-party service currently integrated into Attend75 is Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for advertising.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

10. Data Security

Attend75 is designed to keep the information used by its core features on your device.

Network communication used by third-party services such as advertising SDKs may transmit information according to those services' security and privacy practices.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your Choices

You can choose whether to use optional features such as timetable scanning and notifications.

You can also manage app permissions through Android device settings.

You may remove locally stored Attend75 information by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the application, subject to Android's normal device behavior.

Advertising and related privacy choices may also be available through your device settings and applicable consent mechanisms.

12. Children's Privacy

Attend75 is intended as a general-purpose student attendance and timetable application.

Attend75 does not knowingly request children to provide personal information through an Attend75 account because the app does not require an account or login.

If applicable laws impose additional requirements based on the age of a user or the country in which the app is used, Attend75 will comply with those requirements.

13. Third-Party Services

Attend75 may use third-party technologies and services to provide specific functionality.

These services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

The principal third-party advertising service currently used by Attend75 is Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to Attend75, applicable services, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

When important changes are made, the updated policy will be made available through the app and the public privacy policy page.

The date shown below indicates when this policy was last updated.

15. Contact
