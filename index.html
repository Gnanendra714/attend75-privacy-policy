Attend75 Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains how Attend75 handles information when you use the application.

Effective date: September 19, 2026

1. About Attend75

Attend75 is a personal attendance and timetable management application designed to help students manage attendance records, subjects, timetables, attendance targets, reminders, reports, and related study information.

Attend75 does not require you to create an account or log in to use its core features.

2. Information You Enter

You may choose to enter information into Attend75, including:

  • Your name or profile name
  • Subject names
  • Attendance records
  • Timetable information
  • Classroom or room information
  • Attendance targets
  • Notification preferences
  • Other information you choose to store inside the app

This information is primarily stored locally on your device for the application's functionality.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

Attend75 uses local device storage for information required by the application.

This may include profile information, subjects, attendance records, timetable information, notification preferences, attendance targets, reports, and other application settings.

Attend75 does not require an Attend75 account or an Attend75 cloud server for these core features.

If you uninstall Attend75 or clear its application data, locally stored information may be deleted by Android.

4. Camera and Timetable Scanning

Attend75 may request access to your device camera when you choose to scan a timetable.

Camera access is used for the timetable scanning feature and is not used continuously or in the background.

Images captured for timetable scanning are used to perform the requested timetable recognition process. Attend75 does not intentionally upload timetable photographs to an Attend75 server.

5. Gallery and Image Selection

If you choose to select a timetable image from your device gallery, Attend75 accesses the image you select for the purpose of timetable scanning.

Attend75 does not intentionally upload selected timetable images to an Attend75 server.

You remain in control of which image you choose to provide to the application.

6. Optical Character Recognition (OCR)

Attend75 uses optical character recognition (OCR) technology to help identify timetable information from scanned timetable images.

OCR may be used to recognize information such as days, times, subjects, and room details.

The OCR processing used by Attend75 is performed on the device using the integrated machine-learning functionality.

7. Notifications

Attend75 can provide optional notifications such as class reminders, attendance-related reminders, daily summaries, missed-attendance notifications, recovery guidance, and target guidance.

Notification preferences are controlled by you through the application's Settings.

Android notification permission may be requested when required by your version of Android.

8. Advertising

Attend75 uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements.

Depending on the applicable configuration, consent choices, and regional requirements, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as IP address, interactions with the app and advertisements, diagnostic information, and device or advertising identifiers.

Such information may be processed for purposes including advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and related service operations.

Google's applicable privacy policies and documentation govern Google's processing of information through its advertising services.

9. Information Shared with Third Parties

Attend75 does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when required for application functionality or advertising.

The principal third-party service currently integrated into Attend75 is Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for advertising.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies, terms, and applicable laws.

10. Data Security

Attend75 is designed so that information used by its core features is primarily maintained on your device.

Network communication used by third-party services, such as advertising SDKs, may transmit information according to those services' security and privacy practices.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your Choices

You can choose whether to use optional features such as timetable scanning and notifications.

You can manage application permissions through Android device settings.

You may remove locally stored Attend75 information by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application, subject to Android's normal device behavior.

Advertising and related privacy choices may also be available through your device settings and applicable consent mechanisms.

12. Children's Privacy

Attend75 is intended as a general-purpose student attendance and timetable application.

Attend75 does not knowingly request children to create an Attend75 account because the app does not require an account or login.

If applicable laws or store requirements impose additional obligations based on a user's age or location, Attend75 will follow those applicable requirements.

13. Third-Party Services

Attend75 may use third-party technologies and services to provide specific functionality.

The principal third-party advertising service currently used by Attend75 is Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

Third-party services may have their own privacy policies and terms. You should review the applicable policies of those services for additional information about their practices.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to Attend75, applicable services, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

When important changes are made, the updated policy will be made available through the app and this public Privacy Policy page.

The effective date shown at the beginning of this policy indicates when the current version became effective.
